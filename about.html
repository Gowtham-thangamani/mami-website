About

A quiet companion,
made gently.

mAmI began as a small idea — that mothers deserve a space that listens before it asks anything in return. We're building it carefully, one breath at a time.

A small thought

What lives inside us when no one is looking.

In every hand you hold, in every breath you catch before it leaves, the mother is also the child who once was. mAmI is a soft place to remember that — gently, without performing.

AI that supports the mother — not just the task.

A companion. A reminder. A quiet presence.

Why mAmI was made.

Because motherhood is carried more than it is talked about. Because one hour in the day — the quiet hour between everything you do — deserves a place.

Because so many of you hold more than anyone counts, and have forgotten what asking sounds like in your own voice. mAmI is a place where being is allowed, not performing.

Our mission.

To gently support mothers through AI that understands, listens, and adapts.

mAmI reduces mental load with calm, practical and emotional support.

Our vision.

A world where mothers are supported, not overwhelmed. Where emotional labor is acknowledged, mental load is shared, and support is always present.

Our four pillars.

Support · Empathy · Intelligence · Trust — four principles that guide every interaction.

i.

Emotional support

Motherhood is emotional before it is logistical.

mAmI offers reassurance, gentle check-ins, and emotional safety.

ii.

Intelligent assistance

Smart, not demanding.

mAmI remembers what mothers shouldn't have to, and simplifies daily life quietly.

iii.

Human-centered design

Technology that feels human.

Calm, intuitive, emotionally aware. Designed for real life, not perfection.

iv.

Trust & privacy

Support only works when it feels safe.

Privacy protected. Mother always in control.

Who it's for.

For the mother holding more than anyone counts. For the one running on the second cup. For the one whose love is loud and whose tired is quiet.

For 25, for 42, and for every age that doesn't get talked about. For first-time and fourth-time. For the ones who chose, and the ones still figuring out what they did.

mAmI is made for adults — 18+

What mAmI is.

  • — A quiet companion.
  • — A soft place for the loud hour.
  • — A space to think out loud.
  • — A gentle way to remember everything.

What mAmI is not.

  • — Not a medical app.
  • — Not a therapy platform.
  • — Not a parenting advice service.
  • — Not a productivity tool dressed in pink.
  • — Not one more thing to keep up with.

A small team.

We're a small group, building mAmI quietly. We'll introduce ourselves properly soon — once we've finished, slowly, what we're making.
