Features

Four soft tools,
for the way your day actually goes.

mAmI doesn't try to do everything. It does a few things kindly, when you need them most.

i.

AI chat companion

You're not alone in your own head.

Talk through your thoughts anytime — like a long phone call with someone who has all night to listen. No advice, unless you ask.

  • A companion to talk to, day or night
  • A circle of other mothers, when you want it
  • Family kept in the loop — straight to WhatsApp

ii.

Tasks & reminders

Let go of remembering.

mAmI holds the small endless list — appointments, half-thoughts, the things only you would notice — so the inside of your head can be quieter.

  • Milestones, gently marked as they arrive
  • Vaccines, on your country's schedule
  • Diapers and formula, before they run low

iii.

Calendar sync

Your life, organised gently.

One soft view of everyone's everything. Nothing flashing. Nothing urgent unless it truly is.

  • Meals planned by child and age
  • Routines that fit your mornings
  • Recipes that grow with your little one

iv.

Wellness check-in

How are you, really?

A small daily moment, just for you. No score, no streak, no graph of how you're doing. A gentle question, asked kindly.

  • A mood, logged in a single tap
  • A line of gratitude, when there's a moment
  • Soft reminders to eat, drink, and rest

· A little tour

Everything, in one calm place.

Milestones, vaccines, meals, supplies, the schedule, and a moment for you — each on its own quiet screen.

A few features, doing just enough. When mAmI's ready, we'll write to you.
